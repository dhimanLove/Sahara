## Overview

Sahara is a web-based legal assistance platform built to help families:

- Secure important legal documents
- Verify authenticity instantly
- Generate legal complaints using AI
- Access simple legal guidance in regional languages

<br/>

It focuses on **security**, **accessibility**, and **instant legal support** for ordinary people facing document fraud, property disputes, or legal confusion.

---

<br/>

# Features

<br/>

## Secure Document Vault

Store sensitive legal documents with strong verification and encryption.

<br/>

### What it provides

- SHA-256 hashing for tamper detection
- AES-256 encrypted Firebase storage
- Offline verification support
- Secure access anytime

<br/>

### Supported Documents

- Land Deeds
- Aadhaar Cards
- Marriage Certificates
- Property Records
- Legal Agreements

---

<br/>

## AI Legal Advisor

Voice-first legal guidance powered by Gemini AI.

<br/>

### Capabilities

- Plain-language legal answers
- Voice input and audio responses
- Multi-language support
- Fast and simple legal assistance

<br/>

### Supported Law Systems

- Hindu Law
- Muslim Law
- Bangladeshi Law
- Nepali Law

<br/>

### Example

> "मेरे देवर कह रहे हैं कि घर उनका है..."

Sahara explains legal rights in short and understandable language.

<br/>

## Legal Letter Generator

Generate official complaints and applications within seconds.

<br/>

### Generates

- Complaints to Tehsildar

- Bank-related Complaints

### AI Detection
